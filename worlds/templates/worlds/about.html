About Lorekeeper

Lorekeeper is a worldbuilding organiser designed for writers, roleplayers, tabletop game masters and creative hobbyists.

It gives creative people a single place to gather and develop their fictional worlds, characters and lore, as well as browsing other people's public worlds for inspiration, too!

What is Lorekeeper?

Lorekeeper gives users a structured place to create and manage fictional worlds. Instead of keeping ideas scattered across notebooks, documents and random notes, users can store their worldbuilding content in one organised web application for free.

Users can create worlds, add characters, record lore entries and choose whether their worlds should stay private or be shared publicly for others to browse.

Who is it for?

Lorekeeper is built for creative people who need a simple way to organise fictional settings, story ideas and connected lore.

Writers

Writers can use Lorekeeper to keep track of their characters, worlds, story notes and ideas.

Roleplayers

Roleplayers can use Lorekeeper to create and manage campaign settings, characters and important lore.

Tabletop Game Masters

Game masters can organise their game worlds, characters, notes and campaign ideas in one connected place.

Creative Hobbyists

Creative hobbyists can collect lore, locations, characters and ideas for their worldbuilding projects.

Why use it?

The aim of Lorekeeper is to make worldbuilding easier to manage and free to use. Users can keep related records connected to the same world, making it easier to find characters, lore and story ideas when they need them.

Each world can become its own little corner of the multiverse, with connected characters and lore entries stored together instead of floating loose in the cosmic paperwork drawer.
