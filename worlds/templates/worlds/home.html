Welcome to Lorekeeper

Lorekeeper is a worldbuilding organiser for writers, roleplayers, tabletop game masters and creative storytellers. Keep track of your worlds, characters and lore all in one place.

Build fictional worlds with ease, manage your characters, record important lore, and share selected worlds publicly for others to explore.

What can you do?

Create Worlds

Build fictional settings and store their summaries, genres, conflicts, tones and visibility settings.

Manage Characters

Add important characters to each world and keep their roles, species, personalities and backstories organised.

Record Lore

Store history, culture, magic, technology, politics and other essential lore in a structured way.

Start building your multiverse

Every story starts somewhere: a kingdom, a spaceship, a haunted town, a school for monsters, or one suspiciously important tavern. Lorekeeper gives you a place to keep those ideas connected as they grow.

Create private worlds for your own notes, or share public worlds so other users can explore your ideas for inspiration.
